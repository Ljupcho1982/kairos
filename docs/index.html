What it does

⏳ElectTell it what you're about to do — sign a lease, ask for a raise, start a journey — and when you're free. It scans the real sky quarter-hour by quarter-hour, refines the best candidates to the minute, and hands you your windows.
📖ChapterAnnual profections, firdaria and zodiacal releasing — the traditional time-lord techniques that say which topics are live in your life right now.
📅CalendarImport a .ics file and it grades the meetings you already scheduled, then offers a better time for the badly-timed ones.
⚖️LedgerRate how each elected moment actually went. Kairos compares it against the times you picked yourself and reports the difference with a confidence interval — including when there isn't one.

No black-box "cosmic energy score". The negatives are always shown next to the positives — a score whose costs are hidden is a score nobody should trust.

An honest detail

"Mercury retrograde" is not a universal veto here. The tradition applies it to contracts, purchases, messages and travel — so Kairos does too, and tells you plainly when it doesn't apply to what you're doing.

The void-of-course Moon has two defensible definitions, and they differ by a lot: on 13 August 2026 the traditional reading gives a 43-hour void and the modern one 19, because the Moon squares Uranus in between. Kairos defaults to the traditional seven planets, puts the choice in Settings, and explains the consequence instead of picking silently.
